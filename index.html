Inspect the operating drivers behind the amounts

Cloud compute: budget 100,000 units at $10 = $1,000K, plus $220K support. Actual 110,000 units at $10.50 = $1,155K, plus $250K support. Volume variance: 10,000 × $10 = $100K. Rate variance: 110,000 × $0.50 = $55K. Support mix: $30K. Total: $185K unfavorable.

Labor plan: 49 contractor-months × $20K = $980K. Extensions add 1.5 months ($30K); four October months ($80K) were posted early. Software has no Q3 operating variance in this case; its $55K reported variance is the duplicate accrual.

Q4 base construction: cloud $1,200K eligible services + $240K other cloud expense; labor 53.5 contractor-months × $20K = $1,070K before the cutoff reallocation; software $1,460K quarterly plan + $110K renewal uplift; network $720K; other $540K recurring costs + the $20K deferred project. Total: $5,360K. This is a bottom-up case assumption, not an extrapolation from the monthly ledger.

Inspect the forecast formula

FY = Q1–Q2 actuals ($10,000K) + adjusted Q3 actuals + Q4 base forecast ($5,360K) + labor cutoff carried into Q4 + demand uplift − cloud discount − contractor benefit − seat benefit + implementation expense.

Cloud uplift = $600K × demand growth. Cloud discount = ($1,200K + uplift) × rate reduction × active months ÷ 3. Labor benefit = months avoided × $20K. Seat benefit = seats removed × $0.1K × 3. No probability weighting or statistical model is used.

Q4 commitments are part of the forecast, not an extra cost layer.

05 / PROCESS & ANALYTICAL ACCOUNTABILITY

Make the reasoning inspectable.

A repeatable monthly finance workflow

  1. Match actuals, accruals and purchase-order service periods.
  2. Route exceptions to an owner; retain evidence and adjustment rationale.
  3. Separate timing, accounting corrections and recurring business drivers.
  4. Reforecast remaining periods from commitments and explicit assumptions.
  5. Publish a decision memo with actions, risks and approval gates.

This is a proposed process design. No actual time savings or accuracy improvement is measured by this demo.

Where AI helps, and where judgment stays

AI assisted the prototype's code, layout and draft explanations. The case exposes inputs, formulas and financial treatments so a finance practitioner can challenge the result.

AI should not independently approve accruals, interpret unsupported contract terms, declare savings realized, or replace the accountable finance and operating owners.

Finance-domain framing and review remain central to this work. The prototype uses explicit scenario arithmetic and assumptions; it makes no statistical predictions.
