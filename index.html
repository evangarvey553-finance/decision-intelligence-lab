THE ASSIGNMENT / SEPTEMBER 2026 CLOSE

Northstar Digital Services

You are the finance partner to the technology VP of a fictional subscription-services company with 1,000 employees. Acquisitions and cloud migration have changed both the size and composition of technology spending. Market funding, contracts and forecast submissions must be reconciled before leadership can rely on the outlook.

How the portfolio grew

Fictional annual technology plans: 2023 $12.0M → 2024 $14.5M → 2025 $17.2M → 2026 $20.0M. The 2026 increase comprises $1.2M acquired-market scope, $0.9M net cloud migration and usage, $0.4M contractual rate changes and $0.3M integration work. These explain the plan increase, not the separate $650K forecast overrun. Cloud migration includes both new cloud spend and legacy services being retired.

The decision

Which remaining balances are supported opportunities, which cover costs still to come, and how does that affect the $20M annual plan?

The evidence

Market funding schedules and transfer references, remaining-expense evidence, a nine-month ledger, a control register and a Q4 forecast.

The constraint

Timing corrections are not savings. Contract opportunities remain assumptions until an owner confirms feasibility and approval.

Case assumptions: calendar fiscal year; technology operating expense only; no revenue model, capital expenditure, cash-flow forecast, tax effects, or statistical prediction. Currency is fictional USD, displayed in $000s (K).

Inspect the operating drivers behind the amounts
Cloud compute: budget 100,000 units at $10 = $1,000K, plus $220K support. Actual 110,000 units at $10.50 = $1,155K, plus $250K support. Volume variance: 10,000 × $10 = $100K. Rate variance: 110,000 × $0.50 = $55K. Support mix: $30K. Total: $185K unfavorable.
Labor plan: 49 contractor-months × $20K = $980K. Extensions add 1.5 months ($30K); four October months ($80K) were posted early. Software has no Q3 operating variance in this case; its $55K reported variance is the duplicate accrual.
Q4 base construction: cloud $1,200K eligible services + $240K other cloud expense; labor 53.5 contractor-months × $20K = $1,070K before the cutoff reallocation; software $1,460K quarterly plan + $110K renewal uplift; network $720K; other $540K recurring costs + the $20K deferred project. Total: $5,360K. This is a bottom-up case assumption, not an extrapolation from the monthly ledger.

Inspect the forecast formula
FY = Q1–Q2 actuals ($10,000K) + adjusted Q3 actuals + Q4 base forecast ($5,360K) + labor cutoff carried into Q4 + demand uplift + market funding stress − cloud discount − contractor benefit − seat benefit + implementation expense.
Cloud uplift = $600K × demand growth. Market funding stress = $300K × market demand growth + (legacy overlap months − 2) × $40K. Cloud discount = ($1,200K + uplift + additional market cloud usage) × rate reduction × active months ÷ 3. Labor benefit = months avoided × $20K. Seat benefit = seats removed × $0.1K × 3. No probability weighting or statistical model is used.

05 / PROCESS & ANALYTICAL ACCOUNTABILITY
How I approached the work


The monthly process
  1. Reconstruct market funding from original schedules and corporate transfer references.
  2. Match actuals, accruals and purchase-order service periods.
  3. Route exceptions to an owner; retain evidence and adjustment rationale.
  4. Separate timing, accounting corrections and recurring business drivers.
  5. Reforecast remaining periods from commitments and explicit assumptions.
  6. Publish a decision memo with actions, risks and approval gates.
This is a proposed process design. No actual time savings or accuracy improvement is measured by this demo.
How I used AI
ChatGPT and Copilot help me build and revise analytical work. For this prototype, AI assisted with code, layout and draft explanations. The source, mapping rules and formulas are available to inspect.
I check proposed changes against the case assumptions and test the results. A plausible explanation still needs supporting evidence.
The controls run the published rules and calculations. They do not ask an AI model for an answer.
